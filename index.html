3 · Write Pitch to Save It

Write a persuasive paragraph (6 to 8 sentences) pitching the preservation of a local hidden gem. This can be a real place or a realistic fictional establishment.

🎯 The What-cleft: [What clause] + [is/are] + [the thing you want to emphasize]
“What this community needs is a safe gathering place.”
“What the developer fails to understand is the cultural importance of the market.”

⚠️ Watch the verb: if the emphasized thing is plural, use are — “What stands out are its eclectic design features.”

  • A persuasive paragraph of 6–8 sentences
  • At least 1 grammatically correct What-cleft structure
  • At least 2 vocabulary words from this week's list
  • Make a clear argument, not just a description

4 · Speak ⭐ Deliver Your Pitch

Give the pitch you wrote in Part 3 out loud. Intonation and emphasis: when you reach your What-cleft sentence, let your voice dynamically accent the word What and the main verb is/are, the way real persuasive public speaking sounds. Pronunciation: deliver your target vocabulary cleanly without stumbling over syllables (ec-LEC-tic, pic-tur-ESQUE).
